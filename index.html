Level 1

Cosmic Descent

Survival horror on a haunted spaceship. The creature can hear your real voice.

Solo Unreal Engine Themed Horror Game Jam #18 Win, Mac, Linux

Mission

Make silence the player's main tool. The level has to give you places to stop, listen and hide.

What I built

  • Split the ship into regions and hid 5 ritual items, so you have to cross every area before the final ritual room.
  • Microphone detection: if you make noise in real life, the creature comes for you.
  • Lockers where you hold your breath for close-call encounters.

Level 2

Slaughterhouse

First-person psychological horror. An abandoned slaughterhouse and a masked former worker who never left.

Solo Global Game Jam 2026 48 hours

Mission

Scare the player with pacing instead of jump scares, in a building that tells its own story.

What I built

  • Mask-based progression: some masks give you a small advantage, others open locked paths.
  • The building's past is told through the space: abandoned tools, sounds behind locked doors, scattered masks. No cutscenes.
  • Narrow corridors and look-alike halls, so you never know where the hunter is.

Level 3

Fall Survivor

A roguelike freefall. Every run lasts under two minutes, and you always want one more.

Team of 3 Loot Jam 2026 48 hours Windows

Mission

A short run that gets harder in a way you can feel, and a shop that makes losing feel like progress.

What I built

  • Three worlds that get harder: city rooftops, a forest with tighter gaps, and a dark cave as the final drop.
  • Fall speed keeps rising. Coins buy health, damage reduction, a slower start and more score per second.
  • Game feel: camera shake on impact, wind that gets louder with speed, slow motion on death.

About me

I came to game design through animation. I study Cartoon and Animation at İstanbul Arel University, and this semester I'm on Erasmus+ in Portugal, at Politécnico de Portalegre.

Animation taught me timing and how to guide someone's eye. Game jams taught me to finish things. In a level, I care most about what the player feels and when: where tension builds, where it breaks, and how the space shows you where to go.

I'm also studying AI-Powered Coding at Anadolu University as a second degree.
